## Handoff flow by motion (old GTM Alignment)

Each motion: Marketing activates → Handoff trigger → Sales executes → Shared outcome.

| Motion | Marketing activates | Handoff trigger | Sales executes | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grow Market Share | Display + LinkedIn campaigns target Growth Accounts and Mid-Market whitespace. | MQL reaches SQL threshold — Funding Received signal confirmed. | AE-led outreach, multi-contact account plays, competitive positioning. | Market Share |
| Expand Within Existing Accounts | Workshop + Email nurture surfaces adjacent use cases to Enterprise and Growth accounts. | Cross-Sell Gap signal flags an open category. | Account manager leads the expansion conversation, warm intro to the new use case. | NRR |
| Increase Revenue or Margin | SDR-supported outreach and LinkedIn ABM target Enterprise and Mid-Market. | Multi-Contact Engagement signal — buying committee forming. | Senior AE engages the full buying committee, builds a multi-thread account plan. | Revenue Growth |
| Launch a New Product | Workshop-led early access campaign reaches Enterprise and Emerging pilot accounts. | Executive Hire signal — new leader opens the door. | AE schedules an executive briefing, positions the pilot program. | Pilot Adoption |
| Enter a New Market | Display + social awareness introduces the category to Growth and Emerging accounts. | Market Intent Surge signal — category search activity detected. | New-logo AE prioritizes inbound interest, leads with category education. | New Logos |
| Improve Customer Retention | Email + SDR-supported account-health campaign reaches Enterprise and Growth accounts. | Declining Spend signal — early risk flagged. | Account manager schedules a save call, reinforces delivered value. | NRR |

## Execution roadmap (full lists)

- **30 days, Align:** confirm executive KPIs; validate priority segments; establish baseline performance; align data sources and ownership; identify immediate commercial friction.
- **60 days, Activate:** launch priority signal use cases; activate one cross-channel campaign; enable sales talk tracks and account prioritization; launch executive measurement cadence; test initial investment assumptions.
- **90 days, Optimize:** reallocate channel investment based on return; refine segmentation and opportunity scoring; scale proven activation plays; operationalize marketing and sales feedback loops; present executive performance and next-quarter roadmap.

## Executive decision framework (old Ch 9)

1. Where should we invest? Enterprise and Growth Accounts — highest expansion and acquisition efficiency.
2. What should we stop? Adding top-of-funnel spend into Mid-Market and Emerging beyond current levels.
3. Which segments deserve more funding? Enterprise and Growth Accounts, per the Investment Matrix in Segmentation.
4. Where is pipeline slowing? Opportunity → Closed Won. Win rate sits at 31.9%, below the 38% target.
5. What should leadership do next? Fund win-rate enablement now; protect what's already ahead of plan; revisit at the 90-day mark.

## Channel economics model (old GTM Strategy budget tool)

Diminishing returns per channel: `pipeline(spend) = cap × (1 − e^(−spend / k))`, `revenue = pipeline × winRate(motion, channel)`.

| Channel | cap ($K) | k |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social | 900 | 60 |
| Display retargeting | 400 | 25 |
| Executive email nurture | 250 | 16 |
| SDR-assisted outreach | 600 | 38 |
| Executive workshops | 500 | 32 |
